New Bubble Makers 2025!

Big congratulations to Barry, Catherine, Ocily, Patricia, Jose, who on the 12th Oct just gone, all graduated as CMAS D1* divers.


Building on previous training, the two Petes (Mc and B) and Pat had a full plan in place for two boat dives through the day. It was a really enjoyable and informative session for all involved.


The group completed the two dives over the early afternoon and through to the early evening. It was gorgeous weather to be out on the water around Dalkey Island. However the in-water visibility was a little cloudy. In what could be deemed challenging conditions for new divers, and including dropping off the boat for the first time, the group completed all required tasks to a very high standard and performed exceptionally well.
