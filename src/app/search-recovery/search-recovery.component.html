Kish Search and Recovery Unit


The club has an active Search and Recovery Unit which is a great contribution to the our community. The unit is totally voluntary responding to requests from statutory bodies and local communities to assist in search and recovery operations all over the county and in neighbouring counties. Over the years, members have been called upon on many times to assist in search and recovery missions. It is funded mainly by annual church gate collections and other community wide fundraising events.


In addition to their existing diving experience, each member of the Search & Recovery Unit is specially trained for search and recovery missions.


The team perform regular training exercises as it’s crucial to work as a cohesive unit. On a typical search operation, there are several roles that need to be performed:


  • Coordinator
  • Shore Marshalls
  • Coxswain & Boat Crews
  • Snorklers
  • Divers
