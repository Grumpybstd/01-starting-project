Entry Level Training Programmes


For the curious we offer a no commitment 'Try Dive' Programme. We welcome everyone who wants to try diving, Snorkling and Free Diving and this is with no commitment. Once people decide they would like to progress to the next level we offer a comprehensive suite of entry level training programmes starting with initial certification for people who are brand new to diving and then progressing through a number of grades right up to Instructor and Senior Instructor grades. We also offer a range of specialty qualifications on top of the core diving programmes.


Within the club we have extensive teaching experience with a number of our instructors having taught in other clubs and in other countries. We have a largenumber of instructors who have been teaching for many years and have a wealth of experience to share with students. This is across all disciplines of diving, snorkling and Free Diving.


  • D* Diver Programme
  • D** Diver Programme
  • Nitrox Diver
  • Diver First Responder (First Aid)
  • Snorkler (Entry Leve Grades)
  • Free Diver (Entry Level Grades)

Its worth notiing here that the above are full CMAS qualifications. CMAS was founded by Jacques Cousteau and is a Global cerfication agency so these certifications are recognised in dive centres around the world allowing you to dive whenever you are on holiday.
