Training Programmes


Have you ever watched The Blue Planet and thought ..'I would love to see that for myself, I wonder what it is like to dive'..


Well we love to introduce new people to the sport so we run a no commitment 'Try Dive' Programme for the curious. We have welcomed everyone from students to retirees and all in between!.


You will find this to be a no stress, and fun introductory dive in our pool. It is very gentle allowing people to move at their own pace during the session. The only mandate is that people enjoy themselves - we place a big emphasis in the club on having fun albeit in a fully supervised and safe environment.


For people who want to take it a step further we offer a comprehensive suite of training programmes starting with initial certification for people who are brand new to diving starting and then progressing through a number of grades right up to Instructor and Senior Instructor grades. We also offer a range of specialty qualifications on top of the core diving programmes.


Not least that you will definitely learn to coxn our 7.5mtr club RHIB - the programmes are listed below.

  • Try Dive Programme
  • Diver 1* Programme
  • Diver 2* Programme
  • Diver 3* Programme
  • Instructor Programme
  • Underwater Photography
  • Coxn Programme
  • Diver First Responder Qualification - PHECC

Training is a combination of theory lecture sessions and pool training sessions initially. Students will progress then to confined open water dives and then to full open water dives all under the supervision of senior instructors.


Once qualified you will enjoy our weekly dive trips on our 7.5mtr dive boat (RHIB). We dive Dublin bay and islands like Dalkey, Muglins, Irelands Eye, Lambay Island. The boat trips alone are stunning.


Equipment will be provided negating the need for an expensive initial outlay. Indeed if students then join the club they are then encouraged to not buy gear until they try various equipment options and learn what is best for their physiology, and importantly, their purse! Members in the club facilitate this by providing equipment on loan, similar to when they themselves started and availed of the same courtesy. This for me is an excellent and invaluable benefit of the club diving model.


For more information relating to CFT see their website at www.diving.ie.

A Typical Week


We go swimming in the Guinness Pool on Watling Street on Wednesday nights, between 8 and 9 p.m. We’ve been known to play underwater rugby occasionally, and the steam room and sauna up the top of the pool help us to wind down afterwards… We have run try a dive nights on Wednesdays during the year - between 8-9pm in the Guinness Pool, address above. For details you can contact our training officer. There is no charge or commitment required for this, it is simply a give it a go and see if you like it then you could look at the possibility of taking this interest further.


What you can see on a dive


The most common question asked by people who have a passing interest in diving is “What is there to see down there?” There seems to be a perception that Irish Seas are dull and uninteresting, when in fact quite the opposite is true. Cold waters of the earths seas such as the Irish Sea and the Atlantic ocean are in fact very alive. Granted visibility in Dublin Bay is not the red sea - you can see photos of one of the club trips at the red sea on our site.

Our Affiliation


Kish Sub Aqua Club is affiliated to the Irish Underwater Council (CFT) which is the national governing body for recreational diving and underwater sports in Ireland. The CFT is affiliated to both CMAS (the world governing body for underwater activities) and the Irish Sports Council.


The CFT provides a nationally recognised training and certification system for diver training, and all Kish Sub Aqua Club instructors are CFT qualified.

For more information on the CFT see their website at www.cft.ie.
